# Diana Spencer: Vulnerability as Weapon

## Film Direction

**Palette:** 60% warm paper canvas (`--canvas` cadmium yellow / `--anchor-paper-yellow`) / 30% hairline rules + repeated canvas (no `--surface` token — layer with hairlines and dashed borders, not a fill) / 10% accent: brand-primary ink-blue carries every hero word and the active diagram element; brand-accent red is reserved for the single most violent beat (Scene 5 glitch moment). No surface-token fill — all cards float on the yellow page with `--shadow-pin-standard` only.

**Type:** display tier = thesis-word heroes and scene core phrases (Space Grotesk heavy, tight negative tracking); mono uppercase = eyebrow labels, dates, chapter chips ("1917", "FIELD FILE 03"); body = card copy and step labels (Space Grotesk sentence case); Caveat hand-script = marginal scribble annotations only — never paragraphs. Three-voice contrast mandatory every scene.

**Motion:** All entries `EASE.entry`; heavy intent for large type blocks and diagram panels; snappy for chips and stamps; idle drift `EASE.drift` at the subtle floor (±2-3 px / ±1-2% scale, 2.5–4s cycle period); exits none — scenes hold their final frame. Budget per scene: ONE root-level macro camera move (slow drift / dolly-in / parallax push) + at most 1–2 secondary live elements; everything else rests.

**Ambient:** Warm paper base with sparse `--paper-grain` texture overlay, full-bleed, every scene. No mesh, no scanline, no halftone, no dual-radial swell — pin-and-paper grain only. Safety-pin or stamp motif floats at low opacity as a decorative background layer (one per scene, resting after entry).

**Never (film-wide):** no mesh gradients, no neon-on-black, no glow bloom, no purple-to-blue AI gradients, no bokeh balls, no solid flat background without grain, no card nesting inside cards, no simultaneous multiple primary subjects without declared handoff.

**Transitions:** `blur-crossfade` and `push-slide` only at break seams (2 types, repeated for cohesion). Scene 1 opening needs no prior transition.

**Stillness-before-climax:** Scenes 6 and 11 only — the ungloved hand reveal and the empty flagpole rupture. These are the two payoff moments where a 0.5s comma lands the weight. No other scene stamps this beat.

**Register mix:** Scenes 1/3/8/10/12 — kinetic typography (thesis words, hero claims, quote energy); Scenes 2/4/9 — diagram/abstract-graphics (family tree sketch, corporate org diagram, spy-file schematic); Scenes 5/6/7 — abstract graphics morphing from symbol to action; Scenes 11 — data-viz / object-as-argument (lone flagpole graphic); remaining scenes layer both. No captured assets (`assetCandidates` all empty across all 12 scenes).
